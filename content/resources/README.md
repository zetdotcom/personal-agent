# Resources

Potentially useful knowledge without current committed outcome or ongoing responsibility.

## Use

- Put uncommitted app, software, and writing ideas in `ideas`.
- Put durable synthesized knowledge in `topics`.
- Put source-specific article and video summaries in `sources`.
- Promote resource to Project only when user commits to outcome and next action.
- Move irrelevant or inactive resources to `archives/resources`.

Resources support future action; they are not a dumping ground for raw input.
