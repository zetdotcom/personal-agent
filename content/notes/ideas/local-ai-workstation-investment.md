## Thesis

Purchase a high-memory laptop on credit and use local models and agent workflows to produce content or paid work worth more than the monthly installment. Portability and model capacity matter alongside raw inference speed.

## Options under consideration

### Apple Silicon MacBook Pro

- Unified memory may support larger quantized models and multiple concurrent agents.
- Quiet operation, portability, and battery life suit sustained local workflows.
- Configurations in the 64-128 GB range are the intended target, subject to current pricing and availability.

### CUDA laptop

- A high-end mobile NVIDIA GPU may deliver faster inference for models fitting in VRAM.
- Limited mobile VRAM may constrain larger models despite high compute throughput.
- Candidate classes mentioned in the source include RTX 4090 and RTX 5080 laptops.

## Proposed workflow

Run specialized local agents concurrently for research, drafting, and formatting, reducing recurring API usage while increasing content output.

## Assumptions to validate

- Current machines and configurations available within 20,000 PLN.
- Model size, quantization, context length, and throughput required by actual workflows.
- Local electricity, maintenance, financing, and opportunity costs versus hosted APIs.
- Realistic revenue attributable to the machine rather than assumed content volume.
- Monthly break-even output and a downside plan if revenue does not materialize.

Hardware specifications, product-release claims, and profitability statements in the source are unverified. No purchase decision is recorded.
