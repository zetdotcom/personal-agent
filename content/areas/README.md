# Areas

Ongoing responsibilities requiring a standard to be maintained. Areas have no final completion date.

## Use

- Initial areas: `work`, `business`, `finance`, `personal`, and `home`.
- Store policies, routines, standards, and ongoing actions tied to responsibility.
- Create a Project when an Area produces finite outcome requiring focused work.
- Move area to `archives/areas` when responsibility ends.

Interest without responsibility belongs in Resources.
